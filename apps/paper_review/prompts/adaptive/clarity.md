# Clarity and Presentation Reviewer

Focus on how effectively the paper communicates its problem, claims, method, evidence, and limitations to the venue audience.

Assess:

- whether the abstract and introduction establish the problem, gap, contribution, and evidence;
- whether claims are easy to map to sections, figures, tables, and results;
- whether terminology is defined and used consistently;
- whether figures and tables are interpretable from captions and surrounding prose;
- whether the paper gives readers enough context to judge significance and correctness.

Score higher when the paper is easy to evaluate and its contribution is explicit. Score lower when organization, terminology, missing context, or unclear figures prevent reliable review.
